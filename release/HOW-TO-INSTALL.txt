Vakya: reply suggestions in your own style, right inside WhatsApp
(Android phone: steps 1-4. Laptop with WhatsApp Web: see the end.)

Is it private?
   - Nobody at Vakya reads your chats. The server writes your replies and
     forgets the messages straight away: it never saves or logs them. The
     code is public, so anyone can check: github.com/skandaumesh/vakya
   - You choose the apps: Vakya works in WhatsApp until you tick more (Telegram,
     Instagram, Messages) in the app. Android sends it nothing from any other
     app, and it never reads your bank, photos or passwords. On a laptop it
     only works on web.whatsapp.com.
   - It reads a chat only when you tap the bubble, and it never sends a
     message for you. Your style, saved replies and notes stay on your phone.
   - To write replies, the messages on screen go to an AI service: Groq, or
     Google Gemini for Kannada-English chats and when Groq is busy. Gemini is
     Google's free tier, so Google may use what it receives to improve its
     products, and people at Google may review it. With your own free Groq key
     (see "Good to know"), your chats never go to Google.
   - Don't use Vakya on chats with OTPs, passwords or bank details.

1. Install
   Google blocks apps from outside the Play Store that use Accessibility
   ("App blocked to protect your device"), because scam apps misuse it.
   Vakya needs Accessibility to read the chat and type your reply, so:

   a. Pause Play Protect for a minute:
      Play Store → your profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙ (top right) →
      turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect".
   b. Open Vakya-0.4.0.apk → Install.
      (If it says "install unknown apps is blocked", tap Settings, allow it
      for WhatsApp/Files, and go back.)
   c. Turn "Scan apps with Play Protect" back ON straight away.
      Your phone stays protected; only Vakya was let in.

2. Turn it on
   - Open Vakya → "Turn on in Accessibility settings" → read the note → Agree.
   - Find Vakya in the list (maybe under "Downloaded apps") and switch it ON.
   - Switch greyed out, or a "Restricted setting" message? Android does this
     for every app installed from a file. Unlock it once:
       1. Try to switch Vakya on, so the "Restricted setting" message appears.
       2. Open Vakya → "Open Vakya's app info" (or Settings → Apps → Vakya).
       3. Tap ⋮ (top right) → "Allow restricted settings", and confirm with
          your PIN or fingerprint.
       4. Back in Accessibility, switch Vakya on.
     Samsung, Xiaomi, vivo, OnePlus and Pixel all have the same ⋮ menu.

3. Use it
   - Open any WhatsApp chat and tap the message box.
   - Tap the round Vakya bubble above it → pick a reply → it's typed for you.
     You still press Send yourself.
   - Choose a style at the top: My style · Professional · Short · Gen Z.
   - Write it for me: type what you want to say in your own words
     ("ask him if he's coming tomorrow"), then tap the bubble. You get it
     written 4 ways: My style, Professional, Short and Gen Z. Tap one to
     replace what you typed.
   - Or ask for something to send: type "pickup line", "roast him",
     "bday wish for amma" or "how do I say no politely", then tap the bubble
     for 4 different ideas that fit the chat.
   - If your message is the last one in the chat, Vakya suggests follow-ups
     (a nudge or an update) instead of replies.

4. Make it sound like YOU (optional, recommended)
   - In WhatsApp, open a chat you text a lot in → ⋮ → More → Export chat →
     Without media → save the file (Files / File Manager → Download).
   - In Vakya → "Import a WhatsApp chat" → pick that file.

Good to know
   - You get 40 free AI replies a day. Your own past replies and the built-in
     offline replies are unlimited.
   - Want unlimited AI? Make a free key at console.groq.com/keys and paste it
     into Vakya → "Your own Groq key (optional)" → Save and test.

On a laptop (WhatsApp Web, in Chrome, Edge or Brave)
   1. Right-click Vakya-Desktop-0.4.0.zip → Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere safe
      (not in Downloads, if you clear it).
   2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn ON
      "Developer mode" (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
   3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the Vakya-Desktop-0.4.0 folder
      (the one with manifest.json directly inside).
   4. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin Vakya. Click the Vakya
      icon to open its settings; it should say "Connected".
   5. Open web.whatsapp.com (press F5 if it was already open) and open a chat.
      Click the round Vakya button above the message box, or press Alt+V.
      Pick a reply (or Alt+1 / Alt+2 / Alt+3) and press Enter to send.
      Write it for me: type your idea in the box first, then press Alt+V.
   To sound like you on the laptop too, use "Your style" in Vakya's settings
   with a chat export (.txt) copied from your phone.
