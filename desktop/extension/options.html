Vakya

AI REPLY ASSISTANT · BY ONEZEROLABS
01 HOW TO USE

Open web.whatsapp.com, open a chat, and click the round Vakya button above the message box, or press Alt+V. Pick a reply (or Alt+1/2/3); it's typed for you, and you press Enter to send.

Write it for me: type what you want to say in your own words first, then press Alt+V to get it in every style (Alt+1 to 4). Or ask for something to send: "pickup line", "roast him", "bday wish for amma", "how do I say no politely".


  


  
  

    
PRIVACY

    
Your chats stay yours

    
    
      
  • Nobody at Vakya reads your chats. The server writes your replies and forgets the messages straight away: it never saves or logs them. The code is public, so anyone can check: github.com/skandaumesh/vakya.
    • 
      
  • Vakya only works on web.whatsapp.com. It can't see your other tabs or websites.
    • 
      
  • It reads the open chat only when you click the Vakya button or press Alt+V, and it never sends a message for you.
    • 
      
  • Your style and notes stay in this browser on this computer.
    • 
      
  • To write replies, the messages on screen go to an AI service: Groq, or Google Gemini for Kannada-English chats and when Groq is busy. Gemini is Google's free tier, so Google may use what it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Add your own Groq key below and your chats never go to Google.
    • 
      
  • Don't use Vakya on chats with OTPs, passwords or bank details.
