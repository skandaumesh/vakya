✦ Features

Built for the way
you actually text

Vakya learns your tone, slang, and emoji habits — then suggests replies that sound like you wrote them.

🎯

Your Style, Your Voice

Import your WhatsApp chat exports and Vakya builds a style card capturing how you text — your sentence length, emoji use, tone, and go-to phrases.

⚡

Instant Suggestions

Three reply options appear instantly — labelled by intent like Accept, Delay, or Clarify. Tap one and it's typed in; you still control Send.

🔒

Privacy First

Messages are sent to your own server only when you tap the bubble. The server doesn't store them. Your data stays under your control.

👥

Context-Aware

Set each contact's relationship — friend, client, professor — and Vakya adjusts formality and tone automatically. Save notes about deals, deadlines, and promises.

🧠

Learns Over Time

Vakya watches what you actually send and builds a reply bank of your real responses. Over time, it can suggest past replies instantly without AI.

🆓

Free to Use

Powered by Groq's free tier — about 100 suggestions per day at zero cost. Swap in Gemini, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible API anytime.

⚙ How it Works

Up and running in minutes

No complicated setup. Just a free API key and a USB cable.

1

Get a Free Key

Sign up at Groq and grab a free API key — no credit card needed.

2

Start the Server

Double-click server.bat and the backend starts at localhost:8000.

3

Connect Your Phone

Plug in via USB, run phone.bat, and enable Vakya in Accessibility settings.

4

Tap ✨ and Reply

Open any chat, tap the sparkle bubble, and pick one of three replies.

🛠 Setup

Flexible by design

Choose your AI provider, run locally or on a server. You own the stack.

Default Provider

Groq — free, fast, no credit card. ~100 suggestions/day on the free tier.

Alternatives

Gemini, Ollama (local), OpenAI-compatible, or Anthropic. Just set one env var.

Fallback Chain

Set VAKYA_FALLBACK_PROVIDERS and Vakya auto-switches when quota runs out.

Self-Hosted

A Python FastAPI server you run yourself. Nothing is stored. Deploy behind HTTPS for production.
